### Issues Found: 2 MAJOR, 2 MINOR

#### Issue 2.1: Four-axis schema (A–D) diverges, undisclosed, from the "cuatro ejes" the researcher herself names in both primary sources
- **Location:** Strategy memo §1 (axis table), §2.1, §2.3; `pseudo_code.md` Phase 3
- **Severity:** MAJOR
- **Problem:** The memo derives Axes A–D from the 5 objetivos específicos. But both the thesis plan's Estrategia Metodológica (p. 12) and the Ateneo (§2, verbatim) state the interview guide was built from four axes explicitly: *(1) representaciones sobre la IAG en educación; (2) preocupaciones y expectativas; (3) concepciones sobre el potencial pedagógico; (4) usos proyectados en propuestas didácticas.* This omits competencias digitales and conocimientos previos as named "ejes." The memo never surfaces or reconciles this second, textually explicit four-axis formulation. A thesis committee that has read the same plan/Ateneo could reasonably ask why the methods section describes a different "four axes" structure.
- **Suggested fix:** Explicitly acknowledge both four-axis formulations, state which governs the write-up's methods section, and justify the choice (or reconcile them — e.g., "representaciones sobre IAG" as umbrella object, with competencias digitales/conocimientos previos as OE-required sub-axes not named among the interview-design "ejes").

#### Issue 2.2: `positioning.md` still contains a leftover Abric-era "aula/tareas" fragment
- **Location:** `quality_reports/literature/ia-generativa-docentes-primaria/positioning.md`, "Differentiation axes (actualizados)," item 4: *"Objeto dual: aula + tareas docentes — la mayoría de antecedentes cubre solo un polo."*
- **Severity:** MAJOR
- **Problem:** The strategy memo's §6.2 explicitly recommends dropping the aula/tareas_docentes split, and the Decision Record documents this rejection at length. Yet `positioning.md` — labeled "corrected" and cross-checked by the memo's own §8 Delivery Check — still lists "objeto dual: aula + tareas docentes" as a live differentiation axis.
- **Suggested fix:** Remove or rewrite positioning.md's differentiation axis 4 to match the memo's single-object framing before the writer phase locks in introduction/positioning language.

#### Issue 2.3 (MINOR-MODERATE): The Ateneo's own three-group literature typology is not engaged
- **Location:** Strategy memo §7, §8
- **Severity:** MINOR
- **Problem:** The Ateneo organizes the field into three groups: (1) TAM/acceptance studies, (2) ethics/concern studies (Arce 2023, Anders 2023 — plagio ~65%), (3) critical-imaginaries studies. The memo engages groups 1 and 3 but never names or positions against group 2 as the researcher's own organizing structure.
- **Suggested fix:** Add a short cross-reference in §7 or §8 noting the three-group typology and confirming where each of the memo's four núcleos/axes sits relative to it.

#### Issue 2.4 (MINOR): Encuesta tension under-weighted relative to what the cronograma shows
- **Location:** Strategy memo §6.6, Pre-Strategy Report row on the docx gap
- **Severity:** MINOR
- **Problem:** The PDF's Cronograma allocates "Realización de encuestas" the same two-month window as "Realización de entrevistas" (Mes 4-5), indicating the survey was seriously planned as a parallel data-collection activity. The memo's framing is accurate but slightly softer than the cronograma evidence supports.
- **Suggested fix:** Elevate this to a "must confirm with researcher before writer phase" item, citing the cronograma as additional evidence.
