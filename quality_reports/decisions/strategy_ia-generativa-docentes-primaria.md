## Decision

Se adopta **Teoría Fundamentada (Grounded Theory)** — categorización deductiva a partir de **cinco núcleos teóricos** (representaciones sociales clásicas de Moscovici/Jodelet — objetivación/anclaje; aprendizaje significativo de Ausubel; alfabetización digital docente de Lankshear & Knobel; imaginarios sociotécnicos de Jasanoff & Kim reinterpretados crítico-materialmente por Saura, Lima & Arguelho 2024; y — agregado en Revisión 3/Revisión-4-de-la-memo, 2026-08-01 — concepciones sobre diseño didáctico y uso pedagógico de la IAG de Palamidessi & Gvirtz 1998) + codificación abierta/inductiva + comparación constante, con **imaginarios sociotécnicos tratados como dimensión analítica activa** rastreada dentro del propio discurso docente (no solo telón de fondo teórico), usando un corpus liviano de referencia (Guía 2025, Programa PaideIA; encuesta Educ.ar 2025) para identificar marcadores discursivos del imaginario oficial/nacional, contrastados con el discurso docente vía los códigos `eco_discurso_institucional` / `voz_experiencial_propia`.

El objeto analítico se organiza, para fines de codificación, en **cuatro ejes analíticos (Axes A-D) derivados de los objetivos específicos reales de la tesis** (competencias digitales; conocimientos previos; preocupaciones/expectativas; concepción de la relación IAG-aprendizaje significativo y usos proyectados en propuestas didácticas) — **no** en una división aula/tareas_docentes, que no existe en el plan de tesis real y se descarta explícitamente (ver Alternativa 5 abajo). El quinto núcleo (diseño didáctico) no crea un quinto eje: profundiza el eje/axis D existente (ver Alternativa 8 abajo y strategy memo §1.0).

**Terminological clarification (Revision 2):** los "cuatro ejes derivados de los objetivos específicos" (Axes A-D) referidos arriba son la operacionalización analítica propia de este memo, usada para organizar el plan de codificación/análisis (strategy memo §2). Son **distintos de** — y no deben presentarse como idénticos a — los "cuatro ejes de análisis" del guión de entrevista, nombrados explícita y verbatim en el plan de tesis (p. 12) y en el Ateneo (§2): (1) las representaciones sobre la IA generativa en educación; (2) las preocupaciones y expectativas asociadas a su uso; (3) las concepciones sobre el potencial pedagógico de estas herramientas; (4) los usos proyectados en el diseño de propuestas didácticas. Ese segundo listado no nombra competencias digitales ni conocimientos previos como ejes paralelos — los trata, junto con el resto del marco teórico, como parte del contenido que cada eje explora, no como ejes del guión en sí. Ver strategy memo §1.0 para la reconciliación completa y el crosswalk entre ambas estructuras. La sección de Metodología del paper eventual debe describir el guión de entrevista usando los cuatro ejes reales y verbatim; los Axes A-D se retienen únicamente como la capa de codificación/análisis de este memo, explícitamente etiquetada como tal — nunca como una repetición de los ejes del guión.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Abric structural design (núcleo central/periférico, Vergès-substitute criteria) — the discarded prior design
- **Description:** The design actually used in the superseded 2026-07-28 memo — a structural reconstruction of núcleo central/periférico using qualitative centrality criteria (frequency, connotative force, organizing function, domain stability) as a substitute for Vergès' rang × fréquence, since no free-association/elicitation instrument existed in the data.
- **Why rejected:** This entire apparatus rests on an incorrect premise. Neither the real thesis plan (`Plan_de_tesis_con_imaginarios.pdf`) nor the domain-profile.md (rewritten after review of the actual plan) specifies an Abric structural design anywhere. The thesis plan's own Marco Teórico section explicitly anchors on Moscovici's objetivación/anclaje pair, with no mention of núcleo central/periférico. The thesis plan's own Estrategia metodológica section explicitly names Teoría Fundamentada, not structural SRT. Continuing with the Abric apparatus after this correction would mean actively contradicting the researcher's own documented design in favor of an invented one — this is the single clearest rejection in this record.
- **What would make this viable:** Only if the researcher's actual design were structural SRT — it is not. Not applicable going forward.

### Alternative 2: Núcleo central por método clásico (Vergès, asociación libre + evocación jerarquizada)
- **Description:** Retrofitting the classic quantitative-structural SRT identification method onto the already-collected semi-structured interview data.
- **Why rejected:** Doubly inapplicable now — it was already rejected in the superseded memo on data-availability grounds (no elicitation instrument was administered), and is further moot now that the design itself is not structural SRT at all.
- **What would make this viable:** A prospective redesign with a free-association instrument from the start — not the case here, on either count.

### Alternative 3: Imaginarios sociotécnicos as theoretical backdrop only (not coded in the interview data)
- **Description:** Use Jasanoff & Kim / Saura et al. purely as contextual/theoretical framing in the literature review and discussion, without actively coding interview segments for echoes of/departures from the official imaginary.
- **Why rejected:** The researcher herself posed this exact question in her Ateneo presentation (confirmed verbatim in `Resumen_Ateneo_Tesistas_Ordiz.md` §4, now directly readable — see strategy memo Pre-Strategy Report, Revision 2) and **resolved it in favor of active tracing**. Adopting the backdrop-only option would silently overturn a methodological decision the thesis author already made, and would also weaken the novelty claim (positioning.md identifies "imaginarios sociotécnicos como dimensión analítica activa" as one of the axes distinguishing this thesis from the adjacent literature — Fuertes-Alpiste 2025, Dussel & Williams 2023 — which analyze imaginaries in documents/policy, not in teacher testimony).
- **What would make this viable:** If the researcher revisited and reversed her own resolved decision — not assumed here; if she does, this memo's §2.4 mechanics would need to be removed and the imaginaries núcleo folded back into pure background/literature-review material.

### Alternative 4: TAM/UTAUT/TPACK acceptance framework instead of SRT
- **Description:** Frame the study around individual technology-acceptance constructs (perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, facilitating conditions) rather than social representations.
- **Why rejected:** This is precisely the dominant framework in the closest international literature on primary-level teachers and generative AI (Calleja & Camilleri 2025; Kaplan-Rakowski et al. 2023; Han et al. 2024 via HCI/design framing) — adopting it would collapse this thesis into the existing crowded category the literature review identifies as the status quo, not the gap. This is also the Ateneo's own "Grupo 1" of its three-group literature typology (§3.1) — see strategy memo §8's cross-reference. It also contradicts the thesis plan's own explicit marco teórico (SRT, Ausubel, Lankshear & Knobel, imaginarios), which never invokes TAM/UTAUT/TPACK as a primary lens (only as adjacent/background literature already cited for contrast).
- **What would make this viable:** If the RQ were redefined around individual acceptance/adoption rather than collectively-constructed representations — not the case here.

### Alternative 5: aula vs. tareas_docentes domain split (invented in the superseded memo)
- **Description:** Treat classroom/student-facing IAG use and the teacher's own preparation-work IAG use as two "campos de práctica" of a single representational object, coding every segment with a domain tag and asking whether the (previously assumed) núcleo central is stable or fragmented across the two domains.
- **Why rejected:** Verified directly against both primary sources (thesis plan PDF, interview protocol) that this split does not exist in the real design. The thesis plan's RQ and all 5 objetivos específicos concern the teacher's own representation of IAG's potential *for designing didactic proposals* — squarely the teacher's preparation work, not a dual aula/tareas object. The protocol itself has no block that directly elicits in-classroom, student-facing AI use (Block 6 is about planning; Blocks 4-5 are general beliefs/concerns) — attempting the split would produce a badly asymmetric, unreliable comparison built on an invented empirical question nobody asked.
- **What would make this viable:** A different protocol with a dedicated block on in-classroom use, and a research question that explicitly poses the dual-domain comparison — neither exists here.

### Alternative 6: Full parallel documentary analysis of the Guía 2025 + Educ.ar corpus (heavy, not light, imaginaries corpus)
- **Description:** Exhaustively code the Guía 2025 and Educ.ar 2025 survey report as an independent, fully-analyzed parallel corpus (akin to Dussel & Williams' 220+ document corpus on Mexican digital-education policy), rather than reading them once for a marker list.
- **Why rejected:** Disproportionate to the scope of a master's thesis with 14-15 interviews as its primary data source, and not what the researcher's own resolved design calls for (per domain-profile.md: "usar como corpus liviano de referencia... no análisis documental exhaustivo"). It would also risk shifting the thesis's center of gravity away from teacher testimony (its actual object) toward a policy-document study — a different thesis.
- **What would make this viable:** If the RQ shifted toward characterizing the Argentine national sociotechnical imaginary of AI in education as the primary object (closer to Dussel & Williams' design) — not the case here; the primary object remains teacher representations.

### Alternative 7: Present Axes A-D as if they were the guión's own four ejes, without disclosing or reconciling the two structures (identified as a MAJOR issue by strategist-critic round 1)
- **Description:** The original rebuild of this memo introduced "Axes A-D," derived from the 5 objetivos específicos, and presented them as *the* four-axis structure of the design — without ever surfacing that the guión de entrevista itself already names a different, verbatim four-eje structure (representaciones sobre la IAG en educación; preocupaciones y expectativas; concepciones sobre el potencial pedagógico; usos proyectados en propuestas didácticas — thesis plan p. 12, Ateneo §2).
- **Why rejected:** This is an undisclosed discrepancy, not a substantive error — Axes A-D are a defensible analytic operationalization of the 5 objetivos específicos — but silently presenting them as if they were the guión's own four ejes risks exactly the kind of unexplained inconsistency a committee member who has re-read the plan/Ateneo would flag at defense. Caught by strategist-critic round 1 (Issue 2.1, MAJOR) on a cold-read against the primary sources.
- **Resolution adopted instead:** Both four-part structures are surfaced explicitly and reconciled via a crosswalk (strategy memo §1.0). The guión's real four ejes govern how the interview instrument itself is described in the methods section; Axes A-D are retained, but explicitly relabeled as this memo's own analysis/coding-scheme layer, built on the real ejes plus two OE-required cross-cutting variables (competencias digitales, conocimientos previos). This is not a new analytic strategy — it is a disclosure-and-labeling fix layered onto the Decision above.
- **What would make this viable:** Never, as originally executed (undisclosed). Full disclosure and reconciliation (as adopted) is required regardless of which four-part structure ultimately anchors the write-up.

### Alternative 8: Reject the researcher's mid-analysis proposal to add a 5th núcleo and 3 new Núcleo 4 codes, keep the codebook frozen at 4 núcleos (Revision 3, 2026-08-01)
- **Description:** Having already begun coding real interviews (P2 coded) against the original 4-núcleo deductive scaffold, one option was to decline the researcher's own proposed expansion — 3 new content codes under Núcleo 4 (`imaginario_inevitabilidad_tecnologica`, `imaginario_desigualdad_estructural`, `imaginario_rol_docente_futuro`) and the entirely new Núcleo 5 ("Concepciones sobre diseño didáctico y uso pedagógico de la IAG," 4 codes) — on the grounds that the codebook was already "live" and mid-stream changes risk inconsistency across interviews coded before vs. after the change.
- **Why rejected:** Two independent reasons. First, this is precisely the kind of theoretically-sensitive, data-driven refinement Grounded Theory expects (comparación constante, §2.2/§2.3 of the strategy memo) — the researcher proposed the expansion *because* exploratory coding of the first real interviews surfaced content the original scaffold did not name, and because a design-theory citation already present in the marco teórico (Palamidessi & Gvirtz, 1998) had never been operationalized into codes. Freezing the codebook at this point would privilege procedural rigidity over genuine theoretical sensitivity — exactly the "forcing" failure mode §2.3 warns against, just in the opposite direction (forcing the *absence* of a category the data is visibly calling for). Second, Núcleo 5 fills a real, previously-undocumented gap: the guión's own verbatim 4th eje ("usos proyectados en el diseño de propuestas didácticas") had no dedicated deductive codes at all prior to this expansion — Axis D's "usos" half was analytically thinner than its "concepción" half (seeded by Núcleo 2) purely by omission, not by design intent.
- **Resolution adopted instead:** Accept the expansion, with two mandatory conditions attached (both specified in strategy memo §2.1/§2.3 and `pseudo_code.md` Phase 2/Phase 5): (a) **retroactive re-coding** — any interview coded before the 2026-07-31 expansion (i.e., P1) must be revisited against the expanded codebook, so the provenance-audit trail can show exactly which interviews were coded against which version; (b) **citation honesty** — "Castañeda et al. (2025)," cited by the researcher for 3 of the new codes, was not in `Bibliography_base.bib` and could not be verified when this revision began; it was marked `% UNVERIFIED` everywhere it appeared and added to the strategy memo's Missing Inputs as a pre-writer-phase action item, not silently dropped or silently accepted. **Update, same revision (2026-08-01):** the researcher subsequently shared the full text; the reference has been verified and added to `Bibliography_base.bib` (`Castaneda2025_beyond_tools_power_structures` — Castañeda, L., Postigo-Fuentes, A. Y. & Arroyo-Sagasta, A., 2025, "Beyond Tools, Toward Power Structures: A Critical Review of AI in Primary Education," *Revista Española de Educación Comparada*, 48(extra), 73-95, DOI 10.5944/reec.48.2025.45126). Condition (b) is now satisfied; condition (a) remains an open, tracked action item (see Risks table below).
- **What would make this viable (i.e., what would justify rejecting the expansion instead):** If the proposed codes had duplicated existing codes (they do not — §2.1's distinguishing rules confirm `criterio_contextual_grupo` ≠ `adaptacion_saberes_previos`, and the Núcleo 4 content codes are analytically independent of the existing provenance codes), or if the researcher had not committed to retroactive re-coding (she has, per the codebook's own changelog and this decision's conditions).

## Rationale

The adopted design is the only one of the seven *substantive* alternatives considered (Alternatives 1-6, 8) that simultaneously (i) matches what the thesis plan and domain-profile actually specify (Teoría Fundamentada, no structural SRT, no aula/tareas split), (ii) honors the researcher's own resolved methodological decisions — both the original imaginarios-as-active-dimension choice and the mid-analysis 5th-núcleo expansion, treating both as legitimate, GT-consistent researcher judgment rather than scope creep to be resisted by default, (iii) is achievable with the data actually in hand (14-15 semi-structured interviews, no elicitation instrument, a two-document light reference corpus), and (iv) is honest about its own scope limits (theoretical saturation may not be fully reached; the imaginaries analysis cannot support macro-level claims about "the" national imaginary; the novelty claim is provisional pending two unverified sources; the Castañeda et al. 2025 citation was initially unverified, was not silently treated as settled while unresolved, and has since been verified — see Alternative 8). Alternative 7 is a disclosure fix, not a competing analytic strategy — it ensures the terminology used to describe the adopted design does not create an undisclosed second inconsistency alongside the one this rebuild already corrected (Alternative 5). Alternative 8 documents that the mid-analysis expansion was accepted deliberately, with explicit conditions, not adopted by default or without scrutiny, and that its citation-honesty condition was resolved within the same revision rather than left open indefinitely.

## What Would Invalidate This Strategy

- The real thesis plan or a verified reading of the Ateneo presentation turns out to contradict the domain-profile's summary of the researcher's imaginarios decision (i.e., if she actually chose "backdrop," not "active dimension") — this was the one open verification gap in the original rebuild; **now closed** (Revision 2): `Resumen_Ateneo_Tesistas_Ordiz.md`, a readable plain-text mirror of the Ateneo document, confirms the "active dimension" resolution verbatim (§4) with no contradiction.
- An entire theoretical núcleo (most likely Núcleo 3, alfabetización digital, given its thin direct protocol coverage beyond Block 2) produces so little codeable material that it cannot support even a modest finding — this would not invalidate the overall GT design, but would require explicitly scoping down the paper's claims about that axis.
- The Guía 2025 / Educ.ar marker list turns out, on closer reading, to be too generic/broad to distinguish "institutional echo" from ordinary pedagogical language any teacher might use regardless of exposure to those specific documents — this would weaken (not invalidate) the imaginaries-as-active-dimension claim and should be checked early, before extensive coding, by testing the marker list against 2-3 interviews as a pilot.
- **(Revision 3)** Retroactive re-coding of P1 against the expanded (5-núcleo) codebook is not actually carried out, or the new Núcleo 5 codes turn out, on closer inspection, to substantially duplicate existing Núcleo 2/Núcleo 3 codes rather than capture a genuinely distinct object — either would undermine the specific rationale in Alternative 8 above and should trigger a re-review of the expansion, not silent continuation. (The citation-verification condition is now resolved and no longer an open invalidation risk — see Key Assumption A7.)
