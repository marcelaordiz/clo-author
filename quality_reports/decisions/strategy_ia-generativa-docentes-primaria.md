# Strategy Decision Record: ia-generativa-docentes-primaria

**Date:** 2026-07-28 (REBUILD — supersedes the 2026-07-28 version, which was built on an incorrect Abric-structural/Vergès premise later found not to match the researcher's actual thesis design)
**Decision type:** analytic-strategy (qualitative — repurposed from identification-strategy per domain-profile.md)

---

## Decision

Se adopta **Teoría Fundamentada (Grounded Theory)** — categorización deductiva a partir de cuatro núcleos teóricos (representaciones sociales clásicas de Moscovici/Jodelet — objetivación/anclaje; aprendizaje significativo de Ausubel; alfabetización digital docente de Lankshear & Knobel; imaginarios sociotécnicos de Jasanoff & Kim reinterpretados crítico-materialmente por Saura, Lima & Arguelho 2024) + codificación abierta/inductiva + comparación constante, con **imaginarios sociotécnicos tratados como dimensión analítica activa** rastreada dentro del propio discurso docente (no solo telón de fondo teórico), usando un corpus liviano de referencia (Guía 2025, Programa PaideIA; encuesta Educ.ar 2025) para identificar marcadores discursivos del imaginario oficial/nacional, contrastados con el discurso docente vía los códigos `eco_discurso_institucional` / `voz_experiencial_propia`.

El objeto analítico se organiza en **cuatro ejes derivados de los objetivos específicos reales de la tesis** (competencias digitales; conocimientos previos; preocupaciones/expectativas; concepción de la relación IAG-aprendizaje significativo y usos proyectados en propuestas didácticas) — **no** en una división aula/tareas_docentes, que no existe en el plan de tesis real y se descarta explícitamente (ver Alternativa 5 abajo).

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Abric structural design (núcleo central/periférico, Vergès-substitute criteria) — the discarded prior design
- **Description:** The design actually used in the superseded 2026-07-28 memo — a structural reconstruction of núcleo central/periférico using qualitative centrality criteria (frequency, connotative force, organizing function, domain stability) as a substitute for Vergès' rang × fréquence, since no free-association/elicitation instrument existed in the data.
- **Why rejected:** This entire apparatus rests on an incorrect premise. Neither the real thesis plan (`Plan_de_tesis_con_imaginarios.pdf`) nor the domain-profile.md (rewritten after review of the actual plan) specifies an Abric structural design anywhere. The thesis plan's own Marco Teórico section explicitly anchors on Moscovici's objetivación/anclaje pair, with no mention of núcleo central/periférico. The thesis plan's own Estrategia metodológica section explicitly names Teoría Fundamentada, not structural SRT. Continuing with the Abric apparatus after this correction would mean actively contradicting the researcher's own documented design in favor of an invented one — this is the single clearest rejection in this record.
- **What would make this viable:** Only if the researcher's actual design were structural SRT — it is not. Not applicable going forward.

### Alternative 2: Núcleo central por método clásico (Vergès, asociación libre + evocación jerarquizada)
- **Description:** Retrofitting the classic quantitative-structural SRT identification method onto the already-collected semi-structured interview data.
- **Why rejected:** Doubly inapplicable now — it was already rejected in the superseded memo on data-availability grounds (no elicitation instrument was administered), and is further moot now that the design itself is not structural SRT at all.
- **What would make this viable:** A prospective redesign with a free-association instrument from the start — not the case here, on either count.

### Alternative 3: Imaginarios sociotécnicos as theoretical backdrop only (not coded in the interview data)
- **Description:** Use Jasanoff & Kim / Saura et al. purely as contextual/theoretical framing in the literature review and discussion, without actively coding interview segments for echoes of/departures from the official imaginary.
- **Why rejected:** The researcher herself posed this exact question in her Ateneo presentation (per domain-profile.md's summary — see Pre-Strategy Report in the strategy memo for the caveat that the primary .docx source could not be read directly by this tool) and **resolved it in favor of active tracing**. Adopting the backdrop-only option would silently overturn a methodological decision the thesis author already made, and would also weaken the novelty claim (positioning.md identifies "imaginarios sociotécnicos como dimensión analítica activa" as one of the axes distinguishing this thesis from the adjacent literature — Fuertes-Alpiste 2025, Dussel & Williams 2023 — which analyze imaginaries in documents/policy, not in teacher testimony).
- **What would make this viable:** If the researcher revisited and reversed her own resolved decision — not assumed here; if she does, this memo's §2.4 mechanics would need to be removed and the imaginaries núcleo folded back into pure background/literature-review material.

### Alternative 4: TAM/UTAUT/TPACK acceptance framework instead of SRT
- **Description:** Frame the study around individual technology-acceptance constructs (perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, facilitating conditions) rather than social representations.
- **Why rejected:** This is precisely the dominant framework in the closest international literature on primary-level teachers and generative AI (Calleja & Camilleri 2025; Kaplan-Rakowski et al. 2023; Han et al. 2024 via HCI/design framing) — adopting it would collapse this thesis into the existing crowded category the literature review identifies as the status quo, not the gap. It also contradicts the thesis plan's own explicit marco teórico (SRT, Ausubel, Lankshear & Knobel, imaginarios), which never invokes TAM/UTAUT/TPACK as a primary lens (only as adjacent/background literature already cited for contrast).
- **What would make this viable:** If the RQ were redefined around individual acceptance/adoption rather than collectively-constructed representations — not the case here.

### Alternative 5: aula vs. tareas_docentes domain split (invented in the superseded memo)
- **Description:** Treat classroom/student-facing IAG use and the teacher's own preparation-work IAG use as two "campos de práctica" of a single representational object, coding every segment with a domain tag and asking whether the (previously assumed) núcleo central is stable or fragmented across the two domains.
- **Why rejected:** Verified directly against both primary sources (thesis plan PDF, interview protocol) that this split does not exist in the real design. The thesis plan's RQ and all 5 objetivos específicos concern the teacher's own representation of IAG's potential *for designing didactic proposals* — squarely the teacher's preparation work, not a dual aula/tareas object. The protocol itself has no block that directly elicits in-classroom, student-facing AI use (Block 6 is about planning; Blocks 4-5 are general beliefs/concerns) — attempting the split would produce a badly asymmetric, unreliable comparison built on an invented empirical question nobody asked.
- **What would make this viable:** A different protocol with a dedicated block on in-classroom use, and a research question that explicitly poses the dual-domain comparison — neither exists here.

### Alternative 6: Full parallel documentary analysis of the Guía 2025 + Educ.ar corpus (heavy, not light, imaginaries corpus)
- **Description:** Exhaustively code the Guía 2025 and Educ.ar 2025 survey report as an independent, fully-analyzed parallel corpus (akin to Dussel & Williams' 220+ document corpus on Mexican digital-education policy), rather than reading them once for a marker list.
- **Why rejected:** Disproportionate to the scope of a master's thesis with 14-15 interviews as its primary data source, and not what the researcher's own resolved design calls for (per domain-profile.md: "usar como corpus liviano de referencia... no análisis documental exhaustivo"). It would also risk shifting the thesis's center of gravity away from teacher testimony (its actual object) toward a policy-document study — a different thesis.
- **What would make this viable:** If the RQ shifted toward characterizing the Argentine national sociotechnical imaginary of AI in education as the primary object (closer to Dussel & Williams' design) — not the case here; the primary object remains teacher representations.

---

## Rationale

The adopted design is the only one of the six considered that simultaneously (i) matches what the thesis plan and domain-profile actually specify (Teoría Fundamentada, four núcleos, no structural SRT, no aula/tareas split), (ii) honors the researcher's own resolved methodological decision on imaginaries (active dimension, not backdrop), (iii) is achievable with the data actually in hand (14-15 semi-structured interviews, no elicitation instrument, a two-document light reference corpus), and (iv) is honest about its own scope limits (theoretical saturation may not be fully reached; the imaginaries analysis cannot support macro-level claims about "the" national imaginary; the novelty claim is provisional pending two unverified sources).

## Key Assumptions

| Assumption | Statement | Credibility | If violated |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 — Deductive seeding does not crowd out genuine emergence | The four-núcleo scaffold sensitizes rather than forces the coding (§2.3 of the strategy memo) | Mitigated by mandatory provenance-flagging (`[deductivo]`/`[inductivo]`) and active counter-instance search — a documented procedure, not just an intention | If an entire núcleo generates only template-filling with no elaboration, report this explicitly as a finding about protocol coverage, not force a rich account that isn't there |
| A2 — Imaginaries-as-active-dimension can be operationalized without a full parallel document corpus | A short, iteratively-refined marker list from one close reading of the Guía 2025 + Educ.ar report is sufficient to identify meaningful echo/departure patterns in interview discourse | Consistent with the researcher's own scope choice (per domain-profile.md); bounded and disclosed (§2.4 Step 4 of the memo) | If reviewers/committee require a fuller documentary analysis, escalate to Alternative 6 as a scope-expansion decision, not a silent workaround |
| A3 — 14-15 interviews permit at least code-level theoretical saturation | The cumulative new-codes-per-interview curve flattens before interview 14-15 for most axial categories | Anchored in general qualitative-methods literature on saturation (Guest et al. 2006; Hennink & Kaiser 2022) — **flagged ASSUMED, not verified against this project's own bibliography** (carried forward from the superseded memo's same flag) | If it does not flatten, report explicitly that saturation was not reached and scope the substantive-theory claims accordingly (exploratory, not complete) |
| A4 — The sampling history (10 intentional + 5 convenience-inflected) does not need to be resolved into a single label | Reporting both components honestly is more credible than forcing a single "intentional" or "convenience" characterization | Consistent with domain-profile.md's explicit instruction to document both | If a referee insists the sample must be characterized as one or the other, the response is that mixed-provenance samples are common and reportable in qualitative thesis work when disclosed transparently |

## What Would Invalidate This Strategy

- The real thesis plan or a verified reading of the Ateneo presentation turns out to contradict the domain-profile's summary of the researcher's imaginaries decision (i.e., if she actually chose "backdrop," not "active dimension") — **this is the one open verification gap in this rebuild** (see Pre-Strategy Report, docx not readable by available tools) and should be closed before the writer phase.
- An entire theoretical núcleo (most likely Núcleo 3, alfabetización digital, given its thin direct protocol coverage beyond Block 2) produces so little codeable material that it cannot support even a modest finding — this would not invalidate the overall GT design, but would require explicitly scoping down the paper's claims about that axis.
- The Guía 2025 / Educ.ar marker list turns out, on closer reading, to be too generic/broad to distinguish "institutional echo" from ordinary pedagogical language any teacher might use regardless of exposure to those specific documents — this would weaken (not invalidate) the imaginaries-as-active-dimension claim and should be checked early, before extensive coding, by testing the marker list against 2-3 interviews as a pilot.
