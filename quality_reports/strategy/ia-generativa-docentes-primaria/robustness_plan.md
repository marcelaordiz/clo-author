# Robustness Plan: ia-generativa-docentes-primaria

**Design:** Descriptive/interpretive — Grounded Theory (deductive seed categories from 4 theoretical núcleos + open/inductive coding + constant comparison), imaginarios sociotécnicos as active analytic dimension.
**Date:** 2026-07-28 (REBUILD — supersedes the version built for the discarded Abric-structural design)

Qualitative equivalent of the standard robustness plan: ordered from most-threatening (assumption-threatening) to least-threatening (sensitivity/reporting refinements). See `quality_reports/strategy_memo_ia-generativa-docentes-primaria.md` for full narrative justification of each item.

## Ordered Checks (Most Threatening First)

### Priority 1: Assumption-Threatening (equivalent to pre-trends/first-stage F)

| # | Check | Assumption Tested | Implementation | Expected/Reportable Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theoretical saturation assessment (code-level AND theoretical-level, distinguished) | N=14-15 is sufficient to develop a substantive theory, not just to stop finding new labels | Cumulative new-codes-per-interview curve (code-level); explicit per-axial-category check of whether later interviews added new properties/relationships (theoretical-level) | Code-level saturation plausible before interview 14-15; theoretical-level saturation may NOT be fully reached for all four núcleos — report honestly, per axial category, not as a blanket claim |
| 2 | Negative/deviant case search | Every axial category is not merely majority opinion dressed up as a grounded finding | For each axial category, actively search corpus for contradicting/complicating cases | Deviant cases found and documented explicitly; resolution stated (confirms/qualifies/overturns), not silently dropped |
| 3 | Deductive-vs-inductive provenance audit ("forcing" check) | The four-núcleo seed scaffold sensitizes rather than forces the analysis | Tag every code `[deductivo]`/`[inductivo]` with entry date; check the codebook is not overwhelmingly deductive with no elaboration | A healthy mix of both; any núcleo generating only template-filling with no inductive elaboration is reported explicitly as underdeveloped, not padded |
| 4 | Institutional-echo sensitivity | Reported findings reflect genuine teacher sense-making, not simply repetition of official/national discourse | Classify each key finding involving `nucleo4` with vs. without `eco_discurso_institucional`-tagged segments; trace each marker to a specific reference-document line | If stable either way, strengthens credibility; if a finding collapses without institutional segments, report as a substantive finding (representación aún dependiente del discurso oficial), not hidden |
| 5 | Coverage-conditioning check | Frequency-style claims are not artifacts of uneven protocol administration | Re-run every "n docentes mencionaron X" claim with the actual denominator (participants for whom the relevant block was administered), not the full N | Every reported frequency in the final paper carries its true denominator (e.g., "9/12", not "9/14-15" if only 12 were asked that block) |

### Priority 2: Specification Sensitivity (equivalent to alternative controls/estimator)

| # | Check | What Changes | Implementation | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Marker-list pilot sensitivity | Whether the imaginaries marker list (Phase 1, pseudo_code.md) is specific enough to distinguish institutional echo from ordinary pedagogical language | Pilot-test the marker list against 2-3 interviews before full-corpus coding; revise list if it over-flags generic language | List should discriminate meaningfully; if it doesn't, narrow/sharpen markers before proceeding to full coding |
| 7 | Sampling-wave sensitivity (original intentional N=10 vs. expansion N=4-5) | Whether findings differ systematically between the two sampling waves | Compare axial-category patterns across wave-1 vs. wave-2 participants (flagged in the thick-description table) | Report descriptively; do not claim a causal or definitive difference given the small subgroup sizes |

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Design-Specific Notes

This robustness plan substitutes for the standard reduced-form/structural battery (no pre-trends, no first-stage F, no bandwidth sensitivity apply — there is no treatment, no instrument, no cutoff, and — unlike the discarded prior design — no structural núcleo-central claim requiring lexicometric triangulation). The equivalent rigor concepts are: theoretical saturation (≈ sufficient statistical power), negative case analysis (≈ falsification test), provenance auditing (≈ specification/functional-form robustness against a "forcing" confound specific to Grounded Theory), inter-coder agreement (≈ alternative clustering/inference robustness), and institutional-echo sensitivity (≈ omitted-confound / alternative-mechanism check specific to the imaginaries-as-active-dimension design choice).
